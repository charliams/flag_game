Flag Reveal

How to play

A flag is hidden behind flat grey. Guess any country, and wherever your flag has the same colour in the same place as the hidden one, that part is uncovered for good.

So guessing France against a flag with a white band down the middle uncovers that middle strip — not every white part of the flag. Matching the layout is what uncovers ground, not just sharing a colour. Guesses are unlimited.
