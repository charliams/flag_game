Flag Reveal

How to play

A flag is hidden behind flat grey. Guess any country: every colour its flag shares with the hidden one is unlocked, and those parts of the hidden flag fill in with their real colour. Parts whose colour is still locked stay grey, and neighbouring grey parts blend together — so the design only emerges as you unlock colours. Guesses are unlimited.

The chips below the flag track each colour: ✓ in the answer, ✕ not in it, faded means you have not tested it yet. How many colours the answer has is never shown — work it out.

Flag set

Changing the set starts a new flag. Emblems and coats of arms are drawn as simplified stand-ins, but always in the flag's real colours.
